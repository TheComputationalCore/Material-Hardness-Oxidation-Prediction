# Benchmark: simple models under grouped validation

Generated by `python -m src.models.benchmark`. Every number comes from
out-of-fold predictions in `oof_predictions.csv`; fold detail is in
`fold_metrics.csv` and the decision record is in `selection.json`.

The shipped pipelines are marked **(shipped)**. Challengers use the same
rows and folds, so differences are not caused by different validation.

## Protocols

- **LOCO_current** (primary): Leave-one-current-out: all rows at the held-out current are excluded from training, so the model must transfer to an unseen process setting.
- **LOGO_condition** (secondary): Leave-one-condition-out: all rows for one Material x Current cell are held out. The other substrate at the same current stays in training, so this measures interpolation only.

The oxidation dataset has 18 rows but only six independent Material x Current
cells: the 10 h and 15 h values are exact multiples of the 5 h value. Treat
oxidation metrics as six-condition evidence, not eighteen-sample evidence.

## Selection

Adoption rule, fixed before running: adopt a challenger only if primary LOCO MAE
improves by at least 20%, the improvement appears in at
least 2 folds, and secondary LOGO MAE does not worsen by more
than 10%.

### Hardness

- Decision: **keep_shipped**
- Shipped model: `linear` (primary MAE 26.2500)
- Best challenger: `ridge` (primary MAE 23.1000)
- Ranking: ridge, linear, global_median, global_mean, substrate_mean, current_mean, random_forest
- Rationale: Best challenger ridge: improvement 12.0% is below the 20% threshold; only 1 of 3 folds improve. Keep the shipped model.

### Oxidation

- Decision: **challenger_meets_criteria**
- Shipped model: `random_forest` (primary MAE 4.401e-04)
- Best challenger: `rate_linear` (primary MAE 1.346e-04)
- Ranking: rate_linear, linear_full, ridge_full, linear_time, random_forest, rate_mean, time_mean, substrate_mean, global_mean, global_median
- Rationale: rate_linear reduces primary LOCO MAE by 69.4% (threshold 20%), wins 3 of 3 fold comparisons, and changes secondary LOGO MAE by -47.2% (limit 10%). Recommend review for adoption in a separate change.

## Notes

- Random forests cannot extrapolate beyond the training current range; folds
  that hold out 120 A or 160 A therefore test a known weakness rather than a
  fair interpolation scenario.
- The application only accepts the paper-tested conditions, so these numbers
  describe transfer across the paper's process schedule, not new processes.
- See `docs/MODEL_CARD.md` for intended use and `reports/audit/` for the
  unresolved unit, sign, and independence findings.
